The first computer programmer was Ada Lovelace, who wrote algorithms for Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine in the 1840s.
The term "bug" originated in 1947 when a moth was found stuck in a relay of the Harvard Mark II computer.
The first ever computer mouse was made of wood and was invented by Doug Engelbart in 1964.
The first computer virus was created in 1971 and was called "Creeper."
There are over 700 different programming languages in existence.
The first one-gigabyte hard drive was released in 1980, weighed over 500 pounds, and cost $40,000.
The internet and the World Wide Web are not the same thing — the Web is a service built on top of the internet.
CAPTCHA stands for "Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart."
The Python programming language was named after Monty Python's Flying Circus, not the snake.
The first email was sent in 1971 by Ray Tomlinson — to himself.
